Reading these: split into Attacking and Defensive rather than one combined radar, matching how professional team radars are usually built. Each axis is scaled 0–100 — percentages (possession, pass completion, duels won) plot as their literal value; counting stats (xG, shots, corners, interceptions, clearances) are scaled against the league's highest total this season; "conceded" stats (xG/shots/corners against) run the opposite way, so fewest conceded in the league scores 100. The dashed grey line is the league average for each stat. Goals are left out deliberately — over 7–8 matches they're a noisy outcome, so xG/shots/corners stand in as the underlying process. These describe playing style, not verified quality: nothing here adjusts for opponent strength, so treat the shapes as a picture of tendencies rather than a verdict on ability.
Reading this: every metric at once, with the higher figure in each row highlighted. Highlighting shows which side is higher, not which is "better" — for conceded metrics, lower is usually the favourable direction. Rates are per-match averages or completion percentages computed from all matches with data for that field; possession is the average of each match's possession share.
